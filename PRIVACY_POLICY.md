# Privacy Policy for Color Picker Extension

**Last Updated:** February 1, 2026

## Overview

Color Picker is a browser extension that allows users to identify and copy colors from webpages. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal data.**

This extension does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information or browsing data with us or any third parties.

## Local Storage

The extension stores the following data **locally on your device only** using the browser's built-in storage API:

- **User Preferences:** Your selected color format (HEX, RGB, RGBA, or HSL) and theme preference (light or dark mode)
- **Color History:** Your 8 most recently picked colors for quick access

This data:
- Never leaves your browser
- Is not transmitted to any external servers
- Is not accessible to us or any third parties
- Can be cleared at any time by removing the extension

## Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions solely to provide its core functionality:

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| `activeTab` | To inject the color picker interface into the current webpage when you activate it |
| `clipboardWrite` | To copy selected color values to your clipboard |
| `storage` | To save your preferences and color history locally |
| `scripting` | To enable the color picker functionality on webpages |
| `host_permissions` | To allow color picking on any website you choose to use it on |

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools.

## Remote Code

This extension does not use any remote code. All functionality is contained within the extension package.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties. There is no data to share because we don't collect any.

## Children's Privacy

This extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or the extension, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.

---

**Summary:** Color Picker respects your privacy. It works entirely locally on your device, collects no data, and has no external communications. Your color preferences and history stay on your device and are never shared with anyone.
